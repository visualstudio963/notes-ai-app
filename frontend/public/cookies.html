Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use only what is needed for the app to work (for example, staying signed in or remembering preferences such as theme and language).

How we use them

  • Authentication and session (e.g. tokens in localStorage or similar browser storage).
  • Preferences (e.g. theme, language).

Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time. Some features may not work until you sign in again.
