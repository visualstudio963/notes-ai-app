Standard feature

What you can do with Scan Cam

  • 📸Capture notes from printed pages in seconds
  • 🗂️Upload photos or PDFs from your device

How it works

  1. Open the camera or upload a photo / PDF.
  2. Preview your document, then convert to text.
  3. Save as a note, export, or share.

Supported formats

JPG, PNG, WebP images and PDF documents. Use good lighting for clearer text.
